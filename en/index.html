One team, from start to finish

In Spain the size and shape of a property often differ between the title deeds, the Land Registry and the Cadastre. We find out why and put it right.

Land surveying

GNSS survey of rural and urban plots, boundary staking and georeferenced plans with the GML file required by the Cadastre and the Land Registry.

Cadastre & Land Registry

Graphic validation reports (IVG), building updates in the Cadastre (ICUC), Article 199 procedures and objections to a neighbour's procedure.

Age certificates

Certificates proving the age of a house or building with historical aerial photographs, to register it or to sell it.

Expert reports

Expert reports for court cases about boundaries, encroachments, rights of way and inheritances.

Locating properties

Inherited a plot and nobody knows where it is? We trace it through the Land Registry, the Cadastre and on site.

Valuations

Valuations of rural and urban properties for inheritances, divisions and court cases.
